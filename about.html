About TrueGrade Metals

Metallurgists first. Traders never.

TrueGrade Metals was built by engineers who spent their careers inside nickel-alloy mills — and got tired of watching buyers get burned by trading companies that couldn't read a certificate. For over 30 years we've manufactured Monel®, Inconel®, Incoloy® and Hastelloy® in our own works in Changzhou, China, and shipped it to industrial buyers in more than 60 countries.

Every order gets a named senior metallurgist, an EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate and a guaranteed response within 24 hours. Not because it's good marketing — because it's how we'd want to buy.

What we stand for

Four principles, zero exceptions.

Traceability is non-negotiable

Every heat is ladle + product analyzed, PMI-verified and shipped with EN 10204 3.1. If a certificate ever doesn't match, we replace or refund.

Deadlines are contractual

Your delivery date is fixed at PO acceptance. If we miss it by more than 5 working days due to our production, we cover the expedited freight.

Engineers, not call centers

One named senior metallurgist owns your order from RFQ to delivery. Technical questions answered directly, in English, within 24 hours.

Honest pricing

FOB / CFR / CIF / DAP with packing, testing and documentation spelled out up front. The quote you sign is the price you pay.
