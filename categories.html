Category questions

Before you send the BOM.

Can you supply forms not listed here?

Yes. We custom-produce U-tubes, rings, discs, blocks, cut-to-shape plate, castings and powder-metallurgy materials. Send your drawing with the inquiry and engineering will confirm feasibility within 24 hours.

Which grades are stocked?

Standard grades such as Monel 400, Inconel 600/601/625/718, Incoloy 800/800H/825 and Hastelloy B-2/B-3/C-22/C-276 are stocked or on a frequent smelting schedule. Rare grades carry higher minimum order quantities.

Do fittings match the tube heats?

Yes. Fittings per ASTM B366 can be supplied from matching heats so the full system carries one traceable documentation package — one MTC file for pipe and fittings together.
