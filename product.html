11 — FAQ

Sourcing-agent questions, answered.

Can't find your answer? Our engineers respond to technical inquiries within 24 hours — speak to a metallurgist.

What is the standard length? Can tubes be cut?

Standard: 1 m or 6 m for overseas orders; 12 m / 24 m on request. Cut lengths incur extra cost — specify the method (laser / plasma / waterjet) in your inquiry.

Can you polish nickel-alloy seamless tubes?

Yes. Polished, pickled, sand-blasted and bright-annealed finishes are all standard options. Polished tube ships in wooden cases by default to protect the surface.

Do you supply matching fittings?

Yes — elbows, tees, reducers, caps and stub ends per ASTM B366, matched to your tube heats so the full system carries one traceable documentation package.

What is the lead time?

Stocked heats ship in days. Made-to-order seamless tube typically runs 30–45 days depending on grade and size. A hard delivery date is fixed at PO acceptance — backed by our on-time guarantee.

Seamless vs. welded — which do I need?

Seamless has no weld seam, giving uniform strength and corrosion performance in extreme pressure, temperature and corrosive service. Welded tube is more economical for mild duty. Tell our engineer your service conditions and we'll recommend honestly.

Can you deliver MTC EN 10204 3.1 / 3.2?

3.1 is standard on every heat at no extra cost. 3.2 with third-party witness (BV, SGS, TÜV) is available on request — we coordinate the inspection before shipment.
