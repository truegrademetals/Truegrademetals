08 — FAQ

Before you ask.

How fast will I get a quote?

Within 24 hours on business days — guaranteed. If information is missing we contact you immediately; if an item is outside our supply scope you still get timely feedback instead of silence.

What is the minimum order quantity?

No MOQ on stocked grades, and 50 kg trial lots for qualification orders. Rare or custom grades carry higher MOQs — confirm with your inquiry.

Will I receive material certificates?

Every shipment includes a mill test certificate per EN 10204 3.1: chemical composition (ladle + product), mechanical results, dimensional data and heat number. Third-party witnessed 3.2 certification on request.

Do prices fluctuate?

Alloy pricing tracks nickel, molybdenum and cobalt markets, so yes — which is why a current quote matters: it locks your cost basis instead of budgeting on last quarter's numbers.

How is shipping handled?

FOB by default with your nominated forwarder, or CFR / CIF with freight arranged end-to-end via our long-term logistics partners, sea or air. Wooden-case packing standard for polished and thin-wall tube.

Can you produce non-standard forms?

Yes — U-tubes, rings, discs, blocks, cut-to-shape plate, castings and powder-metallurgy materials. Send the drawing with your inquiry and engineering will confirm feasibility within 24 hours.
