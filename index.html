11 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What nickel alloy grades do you supply?

We stock and manufacture Monel®, Inconel®, Incoloy®, Hastelloy®, pure nickel and specialty grades in pipe, fitting, bar, sheet and wire forms.

Can you ship globally with full MTC documentation?

Yes. Every shipment includes Mill Test Certificate EN 10204 3.1 as standard, with 3.2 third-party witness testing available on request.

How fast can I receive a quotation?

We return certified quotations within 24 hours during business days, often sooner for stocked grades.

Do you cut to size or supply custom dimensions?

Yes. We provide cut-to-length, machined ends, polished surfaces and custom profiles to your drawing or specification.

What industries do you serve?

Our materials are used in aerospace, oil & gas, chemical processing, power generation, marine, automotive and nuclear applications.
