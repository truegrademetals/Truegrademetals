04 — Specify it right

Grade 1 or Grade 2? A 60-second guide.

The #1 cause of reworked POs. Pick the delivery condition now and your quote comes back right the first time.

Grade 1 — Annealed

≤ 600 °C service
  • ✔Higher strength — 827–1034 MPa tensile at room temperature
  • ✔Standard for marine hardware, fasteners, shafts & ambient-pressure equipment
  • ✔The default for most corrosion-service orders

Grade 2 — Solution-annealed

> 600 °C service
  • ✔Better creep & fatigue resistance at elevated temperature
  • ✔Standard for furnace parts, exhaust systems, heat exchangers & aero ducting
  • ✔Softer (290–414 MPa yield) — easier severe forming
